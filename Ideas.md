# Ideas.md - Strategic Capabilities & Innovation Roadmap

This document outlines future capabilities, strategic innovations, and experimental features planned for the executive portfolio.

---

## 1. Executive Search Partner & Board Committee Portals
- **Granular Document Clearance**: Allow assigning search partners access to specific case study bundles with real-time audit notifications when documents are opened.
- **HMAC-SHA256 Token Links**: Self-expiring invitation links with 8-hour windows, preventing secondary link sharing across external parties.

---

## 2. Interactive Zero-Trust Policy Simulator
- An interactive, client-side sandbox allowing visitors to test perimeter access controls, IP deny lists, and role-based policies against synthetic threat vectors.

---

## 3. Offline Progressive Web App (PWA) Capabilities
- Complete service worker implementation caching the core portfolio layout, curated masterclasses, and executive bio for offline reading on mobile and tablet devices during transit.

---

## 4. AI-Powered Executive Assistant
- An interactive assistant grounded exclusively in Munish Dhiman's public articles, patents, and architectural papers to answer questions regarding zero-trust transformations and enterprise identity architectures.
